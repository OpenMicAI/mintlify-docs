The **Zapier** integration connects OpenMic to **thousands of apps** — letting you automate workflows triggered by your AI voice calls without writing any code.

Unlike native integrations (which add tools your agent uses *during* calls), Zapier workflows run *after* calls to sync data, send notifications, update spreadsheets, create tasks, and more.

---

## How It Works

OpenMic is available as a Zapier app. You can use it as a **trigger** (when something happens in OpenMic) to kick off actions in any of Zapier's 7,000+ connected apps.

For example:
- **New call completes** → Add a row to Google Sheets with the call summary
- **New call completes** → Create a lead in Lawcus
- **New call completes** → Send a Slack message to your team
- **New call completes** → Create a task in Asana or Monday.com
- **New call completes** → Update a record in Airtable

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Zapier Workflows">
    In your [OpenMic Dashboard](https://app.openmic.ai/integrations), click the **Zapier Workflows** tab at the top of the Integrations page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Zapier">
    If you don't have a Zapier account, sign up for free. If you already have one, log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a workflow">
    Use the **"What would you like to automate?"** prompt or browse popular workflows. Select **Openmic** as the trigger app and choose any app as the action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your OpenMic account">
    Zapier will ask you to authenticate with your OpenMic API key. You can find this in your [OpenMic Dashboard](https://app.openmic.ai) under account settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and enable">
    Map the fields from your OpenMic call data (summary, caller number, duration, etc.) to the action app, then turn on your Zap.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  Zapier is especially useful for connecting OpenMic to apps that don't have a native integration yet. If your CRM or tool isn't listed in our [native integrations](/integrations/introduction), Zapier can bridge the gap.
</Tip>

## Native Integrations vs. Zapier

| | Native Integrations | Zapier |
|---|---|---|
| **When it runs** | During the call (real-time) | After the call (post-call) |
| **What it does** | Agent uses tools to look up contacts, book appointments, etc. | Automates data sync, notifications, and follow-ups |
| **Setup** | One-click OAuth in OpenMic Dashboard | Configure triggers and actions in Zapier |
| **Best for** | CRM lookups, appointment booking, ticket creation | Post-call workflows, spreadsheets, notifications, unsupported apps |

You can use **both** — native integrations for real-time actions during calls, and Zapier for post-call automation.
